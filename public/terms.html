Handback is provided free and as is, with no warranty of any kind. It is a hackathon project, not a product with an uptime commitment behind it.

Do not store anything you cannot afford to lose

Handoffs are deleted when their retention window passes, and the operator does not guarantee the service, its availability, or that any handoff will still be there when you come back. Download a copy of anything that matters. Every handoff page has a button that does exactly that.

A link is a key

Anyone holding a whole Handback link can read the handoff it points at. There is no account and no other authorisation, so possession of the link is the entire permission model. Share one the way you would share the document itself.

Reporting content

The operator cannot read what you store and does not monitor it. On a credible report of illegal content, the handoff will be deleted. Open an issue at github.com/BraedenBDev/handback/issues with the handoff's id, which is the part of the link before the #. Do not include the part after the #: that is the decryption key, and posting it publicly discloses the contents.
